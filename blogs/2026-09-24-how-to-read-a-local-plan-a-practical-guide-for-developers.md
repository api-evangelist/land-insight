Local plans aren't written to be page-turners. Most run to several hundred pages, are split across multiple documents, and use terms like "strategic policy" and "development management" as though everyone was born knowing what they mean. Yet the local plan is the first thing a planning officer will check your site against, so understanding how to read one is one of the most useful skills a developer or land promoter can have.
