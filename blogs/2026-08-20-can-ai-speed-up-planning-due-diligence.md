If you've ever spent a Friday afternoon cross-referencing a site against flood zones, conservation areas, Green Belt boundaries, and three different council policy documents, you'll already know the honest answer to "how long does planning due diligence take?" is: longer than anyone would like. It's not that the work is complicated, exactly. It's that it's slow, repetitive, and spread across a dozen different sources that rarely agree with each other on formatting, let alone substance.
