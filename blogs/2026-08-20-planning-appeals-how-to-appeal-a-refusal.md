A refusal isn't the end of the road, but it isn't nothing either. Appealing a planning decision costs time, costs money, and - here's the uncomfortable bit most guides skip over - most appeals still fail. So before you fire off a Planning Inspectorate form in a fit of righteous indignation, the real first question isn't "how do I appeal?" It's "should I?" This guide covers both.
