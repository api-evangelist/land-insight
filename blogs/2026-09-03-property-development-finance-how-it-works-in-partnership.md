Most developers lose money on finance long before a brick is laid. This guide draws on insight from Brickflow , LandTech's development finance partner, to explain why - not through bad rates, but through poor visibility, taking the first offer from a familiar lender, putting down far more equity than the deal actually required, and never finding out what the rest of the market would have done. The development finance market has changed significantly since 2008, and the number of credible lenders is now far larger than most developers realise.
